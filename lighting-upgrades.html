QUESTIONS

Common questions

Can you swap old halogen downlights for LEDs?

Yes. LEDs use far less power and run much cooler than halogens. I’ll check the existing fittings and wiring while I’m there.

Can downlights go on a dimmer?

Yes, with LED downlights and a dimmer that are compatible with each other. I’ll match them so they dim smoothly without flickering or buzzing.

Do you install ceiling fans?

Yes, including fans with lights, on standard and raked ceilings.

Can you help plan where the lights go?

Yes. I’ll work out placement around how you use the room, like good task lighting over kitchen benches.
